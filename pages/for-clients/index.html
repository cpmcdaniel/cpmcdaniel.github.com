For Clients

You’re here because something in your engineering organization isn’t moving the way it should. Maybe delivery has slowed to a crawl. Maybe your platform is buckling under legacy decisions. Maybe your teams are talented but not aligned. Or maybe you simply need a senior engineer who can walk into a complex environment, understand it quickly, and start making things better.

That’s the work I do.

I’ve spent more than two decades building and modernizing systems across finance, banking, energy, healthcare, and enterprise SaaS. I’ve led engineering efforts for loan origination platforms, enterprise search systems, distributed data pipelines, and large‑scale modernization initiatives. I’ve been the person who gets dropped into the middle of a mission‑critical project and is expected to deliver clarity, stability, and momentum.

A few lines from my resume capture the pattern:

“Lead engineer for the point‑of‑sale platform supporting residential solar loan origination…” “Technical lead for the enterprise Data Search Platform powering all loan‑ and mortgage‑related applications…”

Those roles weren’t about writing code in isolation—they were about shaping architecture, aligning product and compliance, mentoring teams, and delivering outcomes under pressure.

What I Help Clients With

Engineering Leadership & Delivery

When your teams need direction, I provide it. I help organizations untangle delivery bottlenecks, establish sane processes, and build systems that don’t collapse under their own weight. I’m comfortable stepping into lead‑engineer or staff‑level roles where the work is ambiguous and the stakes are high.

Modernizing Legacy Systems

If your platform is held together by tribal knowledge and aging infrastructure, I help you move it forward without breaking everything in the process. I’ve led modernization efforts across Cassandra, Oracle, Postgres, AWS serverless stacks, and containerized environments.

Architecture & Platform Strategy

I design systems that scale, integrate cleanly, and reduce operational drag. Whether you’re migrating to cloud‑native patterns, integrating third‑party systems, or rethinking your data architecture, I bring the experience of someone who has done it in real production environments—not just on whiteboards.

Developer Experience & Team Coaching

Healthy engineering teams ship better software. I help organizations improve code quality, adopt modern tooling, and build a culture where engineers can do their best work. I’ve mentored teams across languages and paradigms — Clojure, Go, Python, Scala, Ruby, Java — and I’m an early adopter of AI‑assisted development practices.

How I Work

I’m not a consultant who hands you a slide deck and disappears. I embed with your team, understand your constraints, and help you make real progress. My approach is:

  • Practical — I focus on what moves the needle, not what looks impressive in a presentation.
  • Collaborative — I work across product, compliance, operations, and engineering without friction.
  • Calm under pressure - I’ve been the person responsible for systems that cannot fail.
  • Fast to onboard - I learn your domain quickly and start contributing immediately.

Clients often describe me as someone who “does whatever it takes to get the job done” and “builds systems beyond what we envisioned.” Those aren’t marketing lines—they’re the result of showing up consistently and delivering.

Who I'm Best Suited For

You’ll get the most value from working with me if:

  • You have a complex system or workflow that needs to be re‑architected or stabilized.
  • You need senior engineering leadership without hiring a full‑time executive.
  • You’re modernizing a legacy platform and need someone who’s done it before.
  • You want a partner who can think strategically and execute tactically.

If you’re looking for someone to help you ship faster, build smarter, and reduce technical risk, we’ll work well together.

Let's Talk

If you have a project, platform, or engineering challenge that needs a steady, experienced hand, reach out. I’ll help you understand what’s possible, what’s practical, and how to get from where you are to where you need to be.
